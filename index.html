The Tallow Rite

You are a candle-headed prisoner. Fight down through three biomes to the Warden. When your wick gutters out, the Keeper relights you at the start, and only what you invested in cells remains.

Tricks of the trade
Walls
Hold toward a wall in the air to slide down it, then jump off it.
Plunge
In the air, hold Down and attack to slam the ground.
Parry
Raise a shield just as a blow lands to stun the attacker.
Doors
Attack a door to smash it and stun whoever waits behind.
Rally
The orange part of your health bar comes back if you hit back fast.
Scrolls
Brutality, Tactics and Survival boost matching gear and add health.

Wardrobe

Every candle burns differently. Each character has its own look, flame and ultimate ability. Fill the ultimate meter by dealing damage, then press G.

Controls

Pick a layout, or click any slot and press the key, mouse button or gamepad button you want. Changes save automatically.
